Make a month calendar image (day 1 always on Sunday, no year), click days to highlight them, and copy it as a PNG to paste into Anki cards.

- Pick a month (February shows 29 days), then click any day to toggle a red circle highlight.
- Show or hide the month name per image, so the card doesn't give away the answer.
- **Copy PNG** puts a 2× sharp image on your clipboard.
- The month, highlights, and title setting are saved in the URL, so you can bookmark a card's calendar.
