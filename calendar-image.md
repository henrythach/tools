Make a month calendar image (weeks start on Sunday), click days to highlight them, and copy it as a PNG to paste into Anki cards.

- Pick a month, then click any day to toggle a red circle highlight.
- Show or hide the month and year title per image, so the card doesn't give away the answer.
- **Copy PNG** puts a 2× sharp image on your clipboard.
- The month, highlights, and title settings are saved in the URL, so you can bookmark a card's calendar.
